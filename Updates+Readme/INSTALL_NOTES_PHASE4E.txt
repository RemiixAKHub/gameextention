SonarQube Phase 4E — Connect 4 final complexity pass

Built on top of Phase 4D.

Included safeguards:
- Keeps Wordly and Contexto wiring.
- Keeps history.js/Solitaire undo support.
- Keeps all earlier Phase 1–4D fixes.
- Only targets js/games/connect4.js for this phase.

Targeted file:
- js/games/connect4.js

Changes:
- Split board/drop helpers.
- Split win/draw logic into smaller helpers.
- Split scoring/window generation helpers.
- Split minimax and best-move update helpers.
- Split AI turn finishing from column selection.
- Split player turn finishing helpers.
- Split markup and event wiring helpers.
- Preserved public API: init, onKey, reset.

Install:
1. Extract this pack into your extension root.
2. Choose Replace files in destination.
3. Reload the unpacked extension.

Test:
1. Confirm Wordly opens.
2. Confirm Contexto opens.
3. Open Connect 4.
4. Move cursor left/right.
5. Drop pieces with Space, Enter, Down, and mouse click.
6. Confirm AI responds.
7. Play until a win/draw or reset mid-game.
8. Reload popup and confirm saved Connect 4 resumes.
9. Re-run SonarQube.
